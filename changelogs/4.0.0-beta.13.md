# 4.0.0-beta.13 (1.21-1.21.1)

### L1 accuracy (parity)

Root cause for much of L1 vs-vanilla section drift: **WG heightmaps were deferred until after `buildSurface`**, so surface rules saw empty/stale `OCEAN_FLOOR_WG` / `WORLD_SURFACE_WG`.

- Flush deferred heightmaps at **HEAD** of `buildSurface` (before surface rules)
- Rebuild heightmaps at **RETURN** after surface mutates tops
- Match vanilla fluid post-process: check `aquifer.needsFluidTick()` **per block**, not once per chunk
- Treat air via `isAir()` after null→default stone (same order as vanilla)
